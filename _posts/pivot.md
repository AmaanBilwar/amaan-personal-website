## The State

This is for all the curious george's out there asking me what I've been upto for the last couple of weeks. I have been putting in the work, doing long hours at the **sweat shop**. Rewriting a lot of code and prompting a lot more these days.

The initial idea for `CampusOS` was that we would start off with writing software for schools, so that the most important instituitions of the future are foolproof, solidified and structured, most of which is missing from 99% of them. Such a noble cause and we get to make money you must be thinking, I did too. Startups aren't about making money, they are about solving problems. Real problems people want to pay you for. "Hair on fire" problems as YC calls it.

If you watched the news or were on social media in the last 2 months, you know how the future of this country(**India**) and the education sector was(and is being) treated. People who aren't familiar with all this, let me catch you upto speed. Students were fired at with **AK-47s** because they were participating in peaceful protests for better education infrastructure and organizations running it. If that doesn't help draw a mental image in your head about India, I don't know what will.


### People that can change things are... 

Let me start off with an example here. 

 We went to one of the big schools that has multiple branches across India and one recently opened up in Dubai. I'm not gonna name names but you should be able to figure it out if you're still interested. In this school, rather than understanding why & how their time can be used for better things, they are more proud of the fact that they **HAVE** to do **MORE WORK** and they have to learn a bunch of things to even be able to do this work. They told us that they had to undergo training by going to a dedicated "training" center to be trained to sit at a desk and do redundant tasks. **They are being trained like lab rats.** LITERALLY. And when I mentioned solving problems that force you to dedicate your time to do meaningful work over redundant tasks, the person's ego was hurt.


This also reminds me of this one thing Primeagen said "You should not be loyal to your company because they will replace you as soon as they get the chance. You should rather be upskilling, proving yourself and always be on the lookout for the next best step for YOU."

See the problem here?
