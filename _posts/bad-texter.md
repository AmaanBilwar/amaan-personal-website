---

Don't ask me how I'm doing, ask me in a way where it's almost condescending or just sound super forced.

if  you dont wanna know how i'm actually doing, dont bother you know what i mean.

If things don't go your way, you're upset. things don't go my way and you need me there and I'm there. how is that fair. I'm a confrontational personal and I like it. not because fighting cool fighting funny haha he's so awesome says things to peoples faces, but if you genuinely care about a relationship with someone you won't be upset at them for pointing some things they don't like (not necessarily flaws). there are definitely exceptions to this but you get the idea. they want to keep the same bond or better yet make it stronger, they will work on it and expect you to work on things they don't apprecitate that you did or do. 

---
Apparently this is a hot take? it's definitely not for me. and if it is for you, im osrry boss but we won't get along very well, and i know it. I'd like to see this be proven wrong though.
